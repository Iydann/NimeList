Account & Data Deletion

Users of NimeList (developed by Iydain / Willy) can request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email from the Google account email address you used to sign in to NimeList to willy.iedans@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: NimeList Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include your display name in NimeList (optional, helps us verify faster) and state that you want your account and associated data permanently deleted.
Send Deletion Request Email

What Data Will Be Deleted

Once your request is verified, we permanently delete all data associated with your NimeList account from our Supabase backend:

  • Your authentication account record (email address and auth user ID).
  • Your user profile record (display name, Google ID, and profile photo URL).
  • All anime saved in your My List (favorites).
  • All comments you posted on anime detail pages.

Processing Time & Retention

All verified account deletion requests are processed within 7 business days, and you will receive a confirmation reply by email. No personal data is retained in our active database after deletion. Automated infrastructure backups (if any) are automatically purged within 30 days according to standard hosting backup rotation.

Delete Specific Data Without Deleting Your Account

  • Remove favorites: Open the My List tab in NimeList and tap the delete icon next to any anime.
  • Delete comments: Open the anime detail page where you commented and tap the delete icon on your comment.
  • Disconnect Google Sign-In: You can also revoke NimeList's access anytime in your Google Account settings under Security → Third-party apps & services.
