NimeList · com.iydain.nimelist

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NimeList (package name com.iydain.nimelist), developed by Iydain (Willy), collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the NimeList mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect data that is necessary to provide NimeList's core features (signing in, saving your anime list, and posting comments):

Data Category Specific Data Purpose
Account & Profile Info
(via Google Sign-In)		 Display name, email address, Google/Supabase user IDs, and Google profile photo URL (if available). Account authentication, session restoration, greeting you on the Home screen, and showing your name & avatar on comments you post.
My List (Favorites) Anime entries you save to or remove from your personal My List. Syncing and displaying your saved anime list across sessions.
User-Generated Content Text comments you choose to post on anime detail pages, along with timestamps. Displaying community comments on anime pages. Comments, your display name, and your avatar are visible to other NimeList users.

Search Queries: When you search for anime in the Discover tab, your search keywords are sent over HTTPS to the third-party anime metadata API (api.tenrai.org) strictly to return search results. We do not store your search history in our database.

What we do NOT collect: NimeList does not display ads, does not use advertising IDs or third-party ad SDKs, does not collect location data, contacts, phone numbers, health data, or financial/payment information, and never sells your personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your account using Google Sign-In and maintain your login session via Supabase Auth.
  • To store and synchronize your favorite anime list ("My List").
  • To publish, display, and allow you to delete your own comments on anime detail pages.
  • To respond to support inquiries and process account or data deletion requests.

3. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. To operate NimeList, we rely on the following service providers:

  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC): Used strictly for user authentication (requesting email and profile scopes). See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.): Acts as our backend database and authentication provider to store user account records, favorites, and comments. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.
  • Tenrai API (api.tenrai.org) & MyAnimeList CDN: Used to fetch public anime catalog details, synopses, and poster images. No personal account information is sent to these services.

4. Data Security & Encryption

  • All network traffic between the NimeList app and external servers (Google, Supabase, and Tenrai API) is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to account records, favorites, and comments is restricted through authenticated sessions and server-side database access controls.
  • While we apply reasonable safeguards to protect your data, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% immune to risk.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your account profile, favorites, and comments only while your account remains active. You are in control of your data:

  • In-app controls: You can remove items from My List, delete any comment you posted, or sign out directly inside the NimeList app at any time.
  • Full account & data deletion: You can request permanent deletion of your NimeList account and all associated data (profile, favorites, and comments) at any time by following the steps on our Account & Data Deletion page or by emailing willy.iedans@gmail.com. Verified deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy & Content Filtering

NimeList is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We apply a two-layer Safe For Work (SFW) filter to exclude adult-rated anime entries (R+ and Rx). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data through NimeList, please contact willy.iedans@gmail.com and we will promptly delete it.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
